# AR Room Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 5 August 2026_

## Summary

AR Room does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Everything the app does happens on your device.

## Camera

Camera access is required — the app is meaningless without it. The camera feed is the backdrop the 3D character is drawn onto, and it is the input ARCore uses to detect floors and walls.

The video stream is processed on-device in real time and is never transmitted anywhere. The app does not save camera frames automatically — only the photos and videos you capture yourself by pressing the corresponding button.

## Microphone

Microphone access is requested only when you start recording a video, and it is used solely for that video's audio track. Denying the permission does not block recording — the video will simply have no sound. Audio is never transmitted and is not stored separately from your video file.

## Screen recording

For video capture the app uses the system screen-recording mechanism. Android asks for your explicit confirmation every time. Recording starts only when you press the record button and stops on your command. The resulting file is saved to your device gallery.

## Photos and videos

Captures are saved to your device gallery through the system MediaStore, into "AR Room" albums. The app does not read or have access to your other photos.

## Your 3D models

Model files you add through the import button are copied into the app's internal storage so they can be reused. They never leave the device. Deleting a model in the app deletes that copy; the original file on your phone is left untouched.

## Network connections

The app contacts the network only to fetch 3D models from the built-in catalogue and the catalogue itself. These requests transmit the standard technical information any HTTP client sends: IP address and connection details. We do not associate it with you and do not store it.

Downloaded models are cached on the device, so the app works without an internet connection after the first launch.

## Analytics and advertising

The app contains no advertising, trackers, or analytics SDKs, and gathers no usage statistics.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone, children included.

## Full list of permissions

| Permission | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Camera | AR scene backdrop and surface detection |
| Microphone | Audio for recorded video (optional) |
| Internet | Downloading 3D models and the catalogue |
| Notifications | Ongoing screen-recording indicator (Android requirement) |
| Foreground service | Running screen recording (Android requirement) |
