## Full description (max 4000 characters)

```
Point your camera at the floor and an animated 3D character appears in your room. It stays exactly where you put it, casts a shadow, and hides behind your real furniture.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

• Place characters on the floor, a table, or any surface with a single tap
• Drag to move, pinch to resize, use two fingers to rotate
• Switch between the character's built-in animations
• Place several characters in one scene
• Take photos and record video with sound
• Share the result the moment you capture it

YOUR OWN MODELS

Import any .glb or .gltf file from your phone and it joins the carousel. If the model contains animations, the app picks them up automatically.

WHY IT LOOKS BELIEVABLE

The app uses ARCore to read the real geometry of your room. Character lighting adapts to the light around you, and depth occlusion hides it behind a sofa or a table when it stands behind them.

WORKS OFFLINE

Models download once and stay on your device. After the first launch no connection is needed.

NO ADS, NO DATA COLLECTION

The camera feed is processed on your phone and never leaves it. The app contains no advertising, trackers, or analytics.

REQUIREMENTS

An ARCore-supported phone running Android 9 or newer. Google publishes the list of compatible devices.
```
